Family Index

Private family organiser for iPhone · Support & Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 18, 2026

About the App

Family Index keeps a family's calendar, addresses, blood groups and health insurance documents together in one private space. Every family member uses the app on their own iPhone and signs in with their Apple ID. The app also has event reminders and an SOS button that sends your location to a chosen contact through WhatsApp.

Common Questions

  • Starting a family. Install Family Index and sign in with Apple. Without an invitation, a new family is created for you and you become its Admin.
  • Adding a family member. Only the Admin can do this. On the home screen tap + Add, enter the person's name and relationship, and send them the invitation by WhatsApp, Messages, email or QR code.
  • Joining with an invitation. Install the app, then tap the invitation link, or open the app, tap I have an invitation code, enter the code and sign in with Apple. Each code works once and expires after 7 days. If it doesn't work, ask your Admin for a new one.
  • Changing the Admin. Open Family Members, tap the person and choose Make Admin. A family always has exactly one Admin.
  • SOS. Tap the gear icon next to SOS to choose your emergency contact. In an emergency, tap SOS five times quickly. WhatsApp opens with a help message and your current location, ready to send.
  • New iPhone. Your data is stored securely online. Install Family Index on the new iPhone and sign in with the same Apple ID.
  • Deleting your account. Tap Delete at the top of the home screen. If you are the Admin, make someone else the Admin first.

Privacy Policy

Family Index is developed by Balasundaram Iyyadurai. This policy explains how the app handles information.

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, the app receives an anonymous user ID and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address (Apple may give a private relay address instead). The app never sees your Apple ID password.
  • What the app stores. Your family's name, members' names and relationships, invitation codes, and what you add: calendar events, addresses, blood groups, health insurance details and uploaded files. Also the name and phone number you choose as your SOS contact.
  • Where it is stored. Data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage) in Google's data centre in Mumbai, India, and is sent over encrypted connections.
  • Encryption. Each family has its own random 256-bit key. Event details, addresses and uploaded files are encrypted with AES-256 on your phone before they are sent.
  • Who can see it. Only members of your family, inside the app. Events and addresses marked Private can be seen only by the person who created them, not even by the family's Admin.
  • Location, camera and photos. Your location is used only on your phone while the app is open: to show where you are, to fill in your current address, or to add a map link to an SOS message that you send through WhatsApp. The app does not store or share your location. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose a photo of a card or document to upload.
  • Reminders. Event reminders are scheduled on your phone. No reminder content is sent to any server.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data.
  • Deleting your data. You can leave a family at any time. Tapping Delete in the app deletes your account and your data. When a member is removed from a family, their blood group and health insurance documents are deleted too. You can also email us to ask for your data to be deleted.
  • Children. The app is intended for adults managing their family's information and is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
