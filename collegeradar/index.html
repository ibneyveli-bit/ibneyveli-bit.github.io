About the App

CollegeRadar helps students who want to study abroad keep track of university admissions. It shows admission openings, application deadlines, deadline changes and scholarships published on official university websites, lets you search accredited universities and colleges in your chosen countries, and alerts you when your favourite universities announce something new. Every announcement links to the university's own page.

Common Questions

  • Do I need an account? No. Tap Browse without signing in to search universities and read announcements as a guest. Sign in with Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) to save favourite universities and get alerts on all your devices. Your preferences carry over when you sign in.
  • Changing my countries or degree level. Open Settings and tap Destination countries or Degree level. The Home feed and alerts follow these choices.
  • Following a university. Tap the heart on a university's page. You will be alerted when it announces admissions, scholarships or deadline changes, and reminded 7 days and 1 day before its deadlines.
  • Choosing which alerts I get. Open the Notifications tab and tap the settings icon, or go to Settings → Alert settings. You can turn alerts from favourites and from all universities on or off separately. If no alerts arrive, check that notifications are allowed for CollegeRadar in your phone's Settings app.
  • An announcement looks wrong. Always check the official university page using Open official university page, and please email us so we can correct it.
  • Deleting my account. Open Settings → Delete account.

Disclaimer

CollegeRadar provides admission announcements and university-related information for general reference only. Information is collected from publicly available sources and may change without notice. Students must independently verify admission requirements, eligibility, application deadlines, fees, scholarships and other conditions directly with the respective university's official website before applying. CollegeRadar does not represent any university and does not guarantee admission or scholarships.

Privacy Policy

CollegeRadar is developed by Balasundaram Iyyadurai. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can delete it.

  • Guests. If you browse without signing in, the app uses an anonymous ID. It stores only the preferences you choose (see below) so your Home feed matches them.
  • Signing in. If you sign in with Apple or Google, Firebase Authentication receives your account ID and, if your provider shares them, your name and email address. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only receive a private relay address. We never see your password.
  • What the app stores. Only what the app needs: your name (if shared at sign-in), your nationality, your destination countries and your degree level (Bachelor's, Master's or PhD); the universities you save as favourites; your alert settings; your alert history; and, if you allow notifications, a push token for each of your devices so alerts can reach them. We do not ask for marks, test scores, passport details, income, address or phone number.
  • How it is used. Only to run the app: to show announcements for your countries and degree level, to keep your favourites in sync across your devices, and to send the alerts you have turned on.
  • Where it is stored. In Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications). Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules: only you can read your profile, favourites and alerts.
  • Announcements. Announcements and university information come from public, official sources such as university websites and government registers. They contain no information about you.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell or share your data with anyone.
  • Deleting your data. In the app, open Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, preferences, favourites, alert settings, alert history and device tokens. Signing out also stops alerts to that device. Old alerts are deleted automatically after 6 months. You can also email us to ask for your data to be deleted.
  • Children. CollegeRadar is meant for students preparing for university. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
