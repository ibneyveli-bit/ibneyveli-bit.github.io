EV 360 India

Tools for EV owners and buyers in India · Support & Privacy Policy · Last updated: September 28, 2026

About the App

EV 360 India helps electric vehicle owners and prospective buyers in India. It includes calculators for charging cost, charging time, range, running cost, EV vs petrol comparison, trip cost and carbon saved, a charging log, and a search for nearby EV charging stations.

All results are estimates based on the inputs you enter and typical vehicle data. Actual costs, range and charging times vary with driving conditions, tariffs and chargers.

Privacy Policy

EV 360 India is developed by Balasundaram Iyyadurai. This policy explains how the app handles information.

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-in and does not collect your name, email, contacts or other personal information.
  • Location. With your permission, the app uses your device’s location only while it is open, to find EV charging stations near you. Your location is sent to Google’s Places service solely to perform that search. It is not stored by the app or shared with anyone else. You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings; the other tools work without it.
  • Place and route searches. When you use Trip Cost or Nearby Chargers, the places you type and the route you choose are sent to Google Maps Platform (Places and Routes APIs) to find locations and calculate distances. Google’s handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your data stays on your device. Calculator inputs, your charging log and saved charger prices are stored only on your device and are removed if you delete the app.
  • No ads, analytics or tracking. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • External links. Directions open in Google Maps, and some links open the App Store, Google Play or your browser. Those services are governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Children. The app is not directed at children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
